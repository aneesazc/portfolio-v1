About

I make software feel considered. Every transition has a reason to exist — a delay that builds anticipation, an ease that mimics how something would actually move.

Before motion design, I built software myself — APIs, databases, the parts no one sees. That background is why I default to restraint: the best product animation disappears into the product.

Based in Assam, India. Working with SaaS and tech companies anywhere a timeline can be shared.
